Frequently asked questions

What technologies do you use?

We mainly build with React and Next.js for modern frontends, and PHP/Laravel for backend work. We also work with WordPress when a CMS is the best fit, and we use Tailwind/Bootstrap depending on the project.

What types of websites do you build?

We build landing pages, business websites, portfolios, e-commerce sites, and custom web apps (dashboards, portals, booking systems, admin panels, and more).

How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on the number of pages, design complexity, and features. Once we know what you need, we'll send a clear quote with options, no surprises.

Will my site be mobile-friendly and fast?

Yes. We build responsive layouts for all screen sizes and focus on performance so your site loads quickly and feels smooth.

Will my website be SEO-friendly?

Yes. We build with clean structure, proper headings, metadata, fast performance, and best practices for indexing. Deeper SEO support is available on request.

Do you build WordPress websites too?

Yes. We build and customize WordPress sites, improve speed and design, and set things up so you can easily edit content without touching code.
