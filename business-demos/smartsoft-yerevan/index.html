Company profile

SmartSoft is an IT solutions and software development company based in Yerevan, Armenia. Founded in 2017, it delivers innovative technology solutions to clients worldwide.

Its stated vision is a unique approach to every project, professionalism in everything it builds, and scalable, timeless software solutions.

Services include IT consulting, design, and development of infinitely scalable software.

  • Own products: eUniversity, Flight Management System, and Fuel Dispenser Automation.
  • Portfolio lists 15 projects across web development, embedded systems, and IoT and hardware.
  • Public clients include chambers of commerce, insurers, tour operators, academies, and logistics companies.
