About us

What drives us

Baker Tilly Armenia is a trusted leader in accounting and advisory services. We combine local knowledge with the global capabilities of the Baker Tilly International network.

A

Pioneering audit in Armenia

Since 1996, Baker Tilly Armenia has been at the forefront of innovation, proudly holding the country's first auditing license (No. 001).

G

Regional presence

The only accounting firm in Armenia with a regional branch outside Yerevan, located in Gyumri.

N

Global network

Independent member of Baker Tilly International since 2007, spanning 147 territories, 754 offices and 50,400 people.
