Verified context

What the official site confirms

Engineering studio

HZORTECH is an engineering studio based in Yerevan, Armenia, founded in 2022 by engineers from banking, research HPC, and enterprise security backgrounds.

One-system delivery

Product, platform, automation, infrastructure, and security are treated as one platform rather than separate services bolted together.

Direct engineering access

Clients talk to the engineers who design, build, and run the system — from the first call to production.

Five layers

One platform, five integrated layers

01

Product

Websites, web applications, portals, and dashboards designed around the job the customer is trying to do.

02

Platform

Backends, databases, and APIs that connect the product to everything else the business runs. Python and Django by default, typed and tested.

03

Automation

CRM pipelines, workflow automation, and connected devices so teams stop doing by hand what a system should do.

04

Infrastructure

Cloud architecture, CI/CD, migrations, and high-performance compute — defined in code, monitored, and documented for handover.

05

Security

Hardening, monitoring, and detection designed into every layer from day one, not added at the end.

How we work

Forged, not assembled

01 Heat

Assess

Start with the raw problem — what exists, what hurts, what it costs. Measured, not assumed.

02 Shape

Architect

The system is designed before it is built: data, services, infrastructure, and failure modes.

03 Strike

Build

Working software in iterations, with CI from day one. Progress is visible, not just the end result.

04 Quench

Harden

Security, monitoring, and recovery are set into the system.

05 Temper

Hand over

Documented, tested, running in production. Yours to operate, or HZORTECH’s to keep supporting.

Engagements

Three ways to work together

Project

Fixed scope

A defined deliverable, timeline, and price for a specific problem.

  • Written spec with acceptance criteria
  • Documentation and 30-day support
  • One-off price agreed upfront

Retainer

Monthly

Ongoing engineering capacity each month for teams that need continuous improvement.

  • Monthly review and recommendations
  • Defined response times
  • Scope adjustable each quarter

Dedicated

Enterprise

Dedicated engineering time with defined response for regulated or high-uptime environments.

  • Priority access to the full team
  • Compliance documentation support
  • Quarterly roadmap planning
