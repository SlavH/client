Client portal and service request system

The proposed improvement for Moore Armenia is a secure client portal where clients can request services, share documents, track engagement progress, and manage deadlines in one place.

Service request intake

Structured requests for audit, tax, accounting, advisory, IFRS implementation, valuation, and registration support, routed to the right partner.

Document exchange

A single vault for financial statements, tax filings, engagement letters, and working papers, with version and access placeholders.

Engagement tracking

Status, milestones, and owner visibility for each engagement, from proposal to final report, without email chains.

Deadline reminders

Configurable reminders for audit fieldwork, tax submissions, and reporting cycles. Dates shown above are placeholders.

Secure messaging

Threaded questions and clarifications linked to the relevant engagement and document, rather than ad hoc email.

Access control

Role-based access for client finance teams, Moore Armenia staff, and partners. Authentication is a placeholder in this demo.
