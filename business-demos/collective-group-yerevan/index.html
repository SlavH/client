About Collective Group

Collective Group is a restaurant and cafe group founded in Yerevan, Armenia, by a team passionate about good music, comfort, quality, and exceptional design. The group began with the Fresh House juice bar concept in 2008, founded by Artak Harutyunyan, and has since opened six establishments across Yerevan.

Our mission

Collective's mission is to provide an exceptional customer experience while giving people the chance to relax from their everyday hassles and problems — choosing top-quality ingredients, training staff for unmatched customer service, and working with design specialists to make establishments easy on the eye.
