About Aparto

Aparto Real Estate Agency was incorporated on March 27, 2023 in Yerevan, Republic of Armenia, by Alex Babajanian, founding director. After six years of experience in the developing Yerevan real estate market, he created an organization aimed at raising service quality and reducing friction for clients buying, selling, renting, or leasing property.

The team describes itself as becoming a competitor in the Armenian real estate market and influencing marketing and working strategies of other agencies. Its mission is to help individuals and families find apartments, mansions, or business workspaces for purchase or rent, with an individual approach to each client.

Frequently asked questions

Questions are taken from the official Aparto website. The full answer below is reproduced as published.

Why should I sign a Demonstration Agreement before visiting the apartment?

It is necessary to sign a 'Service Provision Agreement' before looking for apartments with the help of Aparto real estate agency, where the rights and responsibilities of both parties are clearly presented. This agreement is signed to insure both parties against further disputes and to specify all the properties that the subsequent buyer has visited with our agent. By signing the agreement, you pay a commission for the service only for the property that you have visited with us and subsequently purchased. Your rights are not limited to other properties, and if you buy another apartment that we have not shown you, you have no financial obligation before us.
