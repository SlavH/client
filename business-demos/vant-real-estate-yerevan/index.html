Verified business profile

Vant Real Estate describes itself as a real estate agency working across Yerevan in sales, rentals, property selection, and full deal support. It also states that its listing database refreshes every 30 minutes.

What the agency does

  • Buying and selling apartments, houses, and commercial property.
  • Long-term rental of residential and commercial property.
  • Property selection matched to district, budget, and requirements.
  • Full deal support from viewing through paperwork.

Mortgage support

  • Rates from 12.25%, terms up to 20 years, in AMD.
  • No fees for issuance, application review, or servicing.
  • Works with Russian citizens.
  • Down payment from 0% for new builds, with up to 50% financed and the rest paid to the developer in installments.
  • Renovation loans up to 20 million AMD and refinancing options.

Market coverage

  • Works across Yerevan districts.
  • Publishes district-level apartment price benchmarks.
  • Lists partner developments in multiple Yerevan districts.
  • Offers a one-minute property request quiz on the official website.

Services presented clearly

The concept groups verified services into clear paths so visitors can quickly understand what help is available and how to start.

Apartments

Sale and rental of apartments in any district of Yerevan, from resale units to new builds.

Houses

Sourcing houses and cottages for sale and long-term rental.

Commercial property

Space for offices, retail, or production, available for sale and rental.

District guidance

The agency states that it works across every district of Yerevan and advises which area fits a client's needs.
