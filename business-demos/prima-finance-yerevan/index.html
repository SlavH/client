The Story of Prima Finance

Prima Finance was founded in 2015 in Armenia with a clear mission: to elevate the quality of financial consulting and accounting services in the local market by introducing international standards and a structured, systematic approach.

Integrity

We uphold uncompromising ethical standards and full transparency in every engagement.

Precision

Client Partnership

Confidentiality

Our Services

Financial consulting and accounting services built on international standards and a structured, systematic approach.

01 · Accounting & Financial Services

End-to-end accounting, bookkeeping, payroll and management reporting, supported by remote and back-office solutions.

02 · Tax & Compliance

Strategic tax consulting, accurate compliance and reporting, VAT and corporate tax management, tax planning and structuring.

03 · Audit & Assurance

Financial and tax audits, alongside detailed reviews of internal controls and regulatory adherence.

04 · Advisory & Corporate Finance

Financial advisory, management consulting, M&A transactions and comprehensive business valuation.

05 · Outsourcing & CFO Solutions

Fractional CFO expertise, financial planning and analysis (FP&A) and full-cycle finance and accounting outsourcing.

06 · Legal & Corporate Services

Corporate legal support, company structuring, compliance advisory, contract and regulatory support.
