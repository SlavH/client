Who we are

AMH Audit combines local, regional, and international experience. The following statements are taken from the firm's official website and are not expanded with invented claims.

Experience

AMH Audit's team comprises specialists with extensive experience across industry sectors in Armenia, the United Kingdom, Russia, Georgia, Kazakhstan, and Kyrgyzstan. The core team includes Armenian auditors qualified as FCCAs, AAAAs, and certified auditors of the Ministry of Finance of the Republic of Armenia, with Big Four audit and advisory experience in Armenia, Georgia, Russia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, and the United Kingdom.

HLB International network

Since November 2013 AMH Audit CJSC has been the Armenian member of HLB International, a network of independent professional accounting firms and business advisers headquartered in London. HLB International was formed in 1969 and is a member of the Forum of Firms. AMH Audit states that, together with HLB, it has reached #9 in the global ranking of accounting networks.

Founder and governance

The founder of the firm, owner of 100% of its share capital, and ultimate controlling party is Mr. Aram Hovhannisyan, PhD, FCCA. Mr. Hovhannisyan is a citizen of the Republic of Armenia. AMH Audit CJSC is an independent auditing and consulting firm providing professional assurance, tax, and advisory services.

AMH Audit and HLB International are separate and independent legal entities.
