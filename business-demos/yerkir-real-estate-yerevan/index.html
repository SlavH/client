Verified business context

The concept is based on Yerkir Real Estate's official website, including its stated history, services, market snapshot, and contact routes.

Company background

Founded in 2004, Yerkir Real Estate Agency states that it is one of the leading real estate companies in Armenia in terms of quality of services and accessibility.

Specialists are described as having more than 10 years of real estate market experience.

Professional standing

The agency states that it is one of the founders of the Association of Realtors in Armenia and holds certificates for all types of services, including Certificate (AG No. 0487) issued by the brokerage services certification agency.

Partnerships

Yerkir states that it cooperates with almost all real estate agencies in Armenia and is ready to cooperate regardless of geographical location.

Market reach

The agency helps clients find real estate in Yerevan and the regions of Armenia, including apartments, offices and commercial spaces, private houses, and land.

Services

Service descriptions are summarized from the official Yerkir Real Estate services page.

Property sales

  • Accurate real estate valuation for apartments, commercial property, or land.
  • Active attraction of potential buyers to the property.
  • Organization of viewings and negotiations with potential buyers.
  • Preparation and processing of transaction documents.
  • Full support at every stage of buying and selling.
  • Assistance with legal procedures until transaction completion.

Property purchase

  • Search for options matching needs and budget.
  • Access to exclusive properties and hidden market opportunities.
  • Viewings organized in a convenient mode and schedule.
  • Professional verification of the legal purity of the property.
  • Referral to partner banks for mortgage programs.
  • Sales contract preparation and comprehensive support.

Investment property

  • Consultation on real estate investment and analysis of client needs.
  • Search and analysis of potential properties by location and value appreciation.
  • Property forecasting and preparation of necessary documents.
  • Advice on financial planning and mortgage or other financial instruments.
  • Legal purity verification and contract preparation.
  • Support at all stages of the investment transaction.

Construction

Yerkir states that its agency carries out construction of new buildings, cottages, and housing estates in city suburbs and beyond, with full-range works and European-standard civil work.

Design support, 3D presentation, and construction documentation are described as available services.

Mortgage credit

Yerkir states that it works with many banks in Armenia and helps clients choose an appropriate credit program, with consultation on mortgage conditions and costs.

Legal and documentation

  • Realtor services.
  • Preparation of documents for purchase, sale, and lease of real estate in Yerevan.
  • Legal support.
  • Privacy and security of transactions.
  • Individual approach and multifaceted attention.
